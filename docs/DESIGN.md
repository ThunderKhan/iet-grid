# IET Grid — DESIGN.md

## Brand
Institutional tech: navy #101b2c, cobalt #315ce5, canvas #f6f8fb, white surfaces. Inter typography and rounded panels.

## Navigation
Overview / Explore rooms / Calendar / My bookings / Announcements / Admin.

## Spatial experience
Two buildings across five floor views. Room selection shows available or occupied status and inspector. Colors are paired with labels. Blueprint-derived placement is schematic, not to scale.

## Temporal experience
Week schedule grid, room-linked events and shared availability state. Calendar selection navigates to room inspector.

## Responsive behavior
Compact mobile navigation, flexible cards and scrollable calendar. Keyboard-focus indicators and explicit form labels.

## Design debt
Replace schematic arrangement with manually verified SVG room polygons when accurate blueprints and allotted time permit. Do not represent placeholders as verified academic records.
